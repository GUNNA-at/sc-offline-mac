# Pictures that come with the launcher

Three photographs from NASA's open archives. Each one was only cut to the shape of the Play page, resized and saved as
JPEG. Nothing else was changed. The `-s` files are small previews of the same pictures for the Settings page.

| File | Picture | Source | Credit |
|---|---|---|---|
| `aurora.jpg` | Earth, green under the Aurora Australis, with the Milky Way rising over the horizon. A long exposure from the International Space Station over the southern Pacific, 29 January 2025. | NASA ID `iss072e574663`, <https://images.nasa.gov/details/iss072e574663> | NASA, Don Pettit |
| `sunrise.jpg` | The first rays of an orbital sunrise on Earth's limb, from the International Space Station above the Atlantic, 12 January 2020. | NASA ID `iss061e123823`, <https://images.nasa.gov/details/iss061e123823> | NASA |
| `cliffs.jpg` | The "Cosmic Cliffs" in the Carina Nebula, taken in infrared light by the NIRCam instrument of the James Webb Space Telescope, published 12 July 2022. | NASA ID `carina_nebula`, <https://images.nasa.gov/details/carina_nebula> | NASA, ESA, CSA, STScI |
